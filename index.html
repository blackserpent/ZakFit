Zakaria's Guide

Weekly schedule

Train on 3 non-consecutive days, for example Monday • Wednesday • Friday.

Warm-up

5–8 minutes easy movement, then 1–2 light practice sets before the first big exercises.

Rest

2–3 min after demanding compound exercises. 60–90 sec for smaller isolation exercises.

Cardio

10–20 min of easy/moderate cardio 2–3 times weekly is enough to start. Keep daily activity up.

Nutrition

Prioritize protein and mostly nutritious foods. Avoid aggressive dieting while learning to train and build muscle.

Recovery

Sleep well and give each muscle time to recover. Consistency matters more than adding extra exercises.
